These notes offer a compelling framework for reading in compact groups, yet existing methods remain fragile, relying on complex multi-term arguments and pre-formed opinions to avoid conversational collapse.

In this work we introduce a marginalia model that annotates stably end-to-end from raw pages, using only two note types: a highlight and a question. This reduces tunable disagreement from six terms to one, while remaining competitive across diverse reading tasks.
